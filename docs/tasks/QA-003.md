## QA implementation and verification, 2026-10-08

The assigned test files now cover fixed-phase jitter and mid-window restart spacing
for each feed; the original October 7 measured 36.982839-second gap remains marked
as a failure, while v2 and v3 timing calculations are explicitly counterfactual.
Strict 35/35/65 limits and the existing failed/outage/quarantine coverage checks are
retained.

Additional deterministic cases exercise delayed executor start, separation of
HTTP/receipt timing from return-only archive persistence timing, three-attempt
Retry-After handling, feed-only deferral until the provider deadline, GTFS/outbox/
telemetry stalls, coalesced status and retained diagnostic gaps, outbox PostgreSQL
limits and cooldown, and immutable pending-capture recovery at both journal stages.
Invalid protobuf claims, mismatched event/entity metadata, unsafe POSIX/Windows
paths, payload/identity mismatches, and external symlinks remain visible without
rewriting committed receipt metadata. The temporary fixtures use canonical source
URLs and do not read or modify the original recording archive.

The recovery cases also interrupt duplicate publication after its durable pending
envelope, vary the retry annotation while retaining the committed row as authority,
and verify that recovery clears the envelope without changing original files or
creating another outbox item. A bounded two-ID concurrency case confirms distinct
captures can progress concurrently and the process-local lock registry is cleaned
up after both complete. The existing 20-thread same-ID archive replay regression
was included in the final focused run without modification.

The noon race was reproduced before the implementation gate: a tick observed at
11:59:59 could otherwise submit after 12:00. The final tests require zero ordinary
feed submissions after the actual boundary while preserving `force=True` captures.
Pending recovery tests also require a non-null GTFS digest to identify a valid
schedule receipt available by the feed's original receipt time; a missing or later
schedule leaves the pending envelope visible and does not insert a feed row.
