## Escalation issues

- The small pilot and alternative bus services may keep every query within 60 minutes even after broad rail cancellation. That is a valid result. Delay measures address the coarse threshold; do not lower the threshold, remove working alternatives or select the incident after recovery outcomes to obtain restoration.
- Rail platform walking coverage is incomplete and uncalibrated. A baseline-unavailable platform contributes no newly lost journey when the rail closure is applied. Do not fabricate platform connectors, street links, source service or relief routes to force exposure/recovery.
- Candidate generation ranks geographic affected-anchor coverage before evaluating marginal recovery. The phased case-study adapter narrows the source selection to three topology variants in exchange for four activation phases per pair. This can exclude useful bus patterns or retain weak ones; it is not an exhaustive route/phase/fleet design. The source-timing variant and phase grid also constrain the apparent resource frontier. Case-study outcomes must be labelled conditional on this candidate set. Any later selector expansion, activation search or extra duty option requires a separately versioned approved development comparison, not a post-confirmation repair.
- The original busiest-affected-bus-pattern comparator is naturally null for a pure rail cancellation because its bus source trips are unaffected. The supplementary source-frequency policy requires a documented separate interface and actual selected IDs; do not manufacture an engine comparison result.
- The existing engine's all-primary duration objective may favor improvement to queries unaffected by the closure. Report this decomposition and the impacted cohort alongside the objective; do not claim all objective reduction reverses disruption harm.
- Whole-trip cancellation extends beyond the physical section/time window. If the project instead needs short-turn operation or segment-only service, that is a new scenario contract requiring review, not a wording adjustment.
- Initial snapshot enumeration accidentally included analytical JSON arrays; the read-only inspection was corrected to immutable snapshot filenames. No repeated algorithm failure or source mutation occurred.
